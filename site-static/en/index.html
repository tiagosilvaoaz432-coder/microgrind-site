Common questions

Still have questions? Write to us.

ola@microgrind.app
Is it a mind map?

No. A mind map is a free-form visual diagram. A Subject Map organises your subject into topics, concepts, definitions and how they connect, using your own material. MicroGrind uses that structure to prepare short study sessions.

Does it work with dense textbooks and slides that were rushed through?

That’s what we designed it for. The map separates the material into chapters, topics and concepts so you can see how it fits together before you study.

I have little time and several subjects to study. Is this for me?

Sessions last between 5 and 25 minutes and fit between classes. When you start a session, you choose its duration to study the material organised in your Subject Map.

What can I upload?

PDFs, Word files, text and photos of your notes. The Subject Map is built from the file you upload.

Can I use it?

Yes. Open it on the web, or install it on your phone.

How much does it cost?

€4 a month. See how MicroGrind compares with other study apps on the Compare page.
