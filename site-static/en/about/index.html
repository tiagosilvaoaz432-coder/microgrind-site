About MicroGrind

MicroGrind is a study app that builds a Subject Map — topics, concepts, definitions and how they connect — from your own notes, textbook or slides, then has you study it in 5–25 minute sessions. Built for any course. Short sessions. Real progress.

What it is

MicroGrind builds a Subject Map from the materials you already study with, then organises the work into short sessions. Short sessions. Real progress.

Who it is for

For students at any level — primary, secondary or higher education — in any course or subject. Your Subject Map is built from your own notes, textbook or slides.

  • Primary
  • Secondary
  • Higher education

Any course or subject — from your own material.

How it works

  1. Upload the material: PDF, notes, textbook or slides.
  2. See the Subject Map: topics, concepts, definitions and relations.
  3. When you start a session, choose a duration between 5 and 25 minutes and study the material organised in your Subject Map.
