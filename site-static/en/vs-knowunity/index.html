MicroGrind vs Knowunity — MicroGrind advantages

MicroGrind is a study app that builds a Subject Map — topics, concepts, definitions and relations — from your course notes, handbook or slides, then runs short study sessions of 5 to 25 minutes. It works for any course or subject, from primary school to higher education.

This page shows what MicroGrind has that Knowunity does not, using verifiable product facts.

What Knowunity does well

  • Community notes, quizzes and flashcards — strong for secondary school and exam prep with peers.
  • AI companion, study plans and mock exams in the app ecosystem (includes an AI tutor — MicroGrind is not competing on that axis on this page).

Choose MicroGrind if…

  • You want a Subject Map built from your material, not mainly other students’ notes.
  • You prefer sessions of 5–25 minutes tied to that structure.
  • You want the subject organised (topics → concepts → relations) before cards or quizzes.

Common questions

Is MicroGrind a mind map?

No. It is a Subject Map: structure of the subject from what you upload. Knowunity centres on community notes, plans and in-app practice.

Can I use both?

Yes. Structure in MicroGrind; community notes or plans in Knowunity if they help.

Does MicroGrind replace Knowunity?

We do not present it as a replacement. This page highlights map + short sessions from the map — MicroGrind’s differentiator.

Any course?

Yes — primary through higher education, any subject.

Which files?

PDF, Word, text, photos. Web + PWA («Coming soon on Android»).

Cost?

MicroGrind: N/A. Knowunity: free core with limits; Pro weekly/monthly/annual — confirm on local checkout.
