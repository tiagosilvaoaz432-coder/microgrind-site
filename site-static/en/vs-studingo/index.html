What Studingo does well

  • Turns notes and textbooks into practice tests and flashcards with AI, with study mode and exam mode.
  • A Free Forever plan with clear limits; Portuguese locale at studingo.io/pt/.

Choose MicroGrind if…

  • You want a Subject Map first (structure of the subject), not only a generated set of cards or tests.
  • You prefer fixed sessions of 5, 10, 15 or 25 minutes tied to that map.
  • You want to see topics, concepts and relations before practising cards.

Common questions

Is MicroGrind a mind map?

No. A Subject Map is the structure of your subject (topics, concepts, definitions, relations) from your material — not a free-form diagram. Studingo mainly generates flashcards and practice tests.

Can I use both?

Yes. You can build structure in MicroGrind and still generate extra card sets elsewhere if you want.

Does MicroGrind replace Studingo?

We do not present it as a replacement. It fits map + short sessions from the map. Studingo fits generating and practising flashcards/tests from notes.

Any course?

Yes — primary through higher education. The map starts from what you upload.

Which files?

PDF, Word, text and photos of notes. Web + PWA («Coming soon on Android»).

Cost?

MicroGrind: N/A on the public site. Studingo: Free Forever with limits; Premium monthly/annual — confirm on studingo.io/pricing.
