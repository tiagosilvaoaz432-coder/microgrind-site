MicroGrind vs Astra AI — MicroGrind advantages

MicroGrind is a study app that builds a Subject Map — topics, concepts, definitions and relations — from your course notes, handbook or slides, then runs short study sessions of 5 to 25 minutes. It works for any course or subject, from primary school to higher education.

This page compares MicroGrind with Astra AI (Study & Exam Prep at astra-ai.co) — not crypto projects or other “Astra AI” names. It shows what MicroGrind has that Astra AI does not, using verifiable product facts. MicroGrind is not positioned here as an AI tutor.

What Astra AI does well

  • Exam Prep from your materials: plan, lessons, quizzes, flashcards, podcasts and mock exams in one place.
  • Web + iOS + Android; PT locale at astra-ai.co/pt/ — strong if you want several practice formats up to the exam date.

Choose MicroGrind if…

  • You want a Subject Map first (structure of the subject), not mainly an Exam Prep plan or explanation chat.
  • You prefer fixed sessions of 5, 10, 15 or 25 minutes tied to that map.
  • You want to see topics, concepts and relations before quizzes, flashcards or mocks.

Common questions

Is this the same Astra AI as crypto / other sites?

No. This page only covers Astra AI: Study & Exam Prep at astra-ai.co. Other “Astra AI” / AstraAI products are out of scope.

Is MicroGrind a mind map / does it have a tutor like Astra?

It is not a mind map. And we do not list “tutor” as a MicroGrind feature on this page (nor on the live site). The differentiator is Subject Map + short sessions from the map.

Can I use both?

Yes. Structure and sessions in MicroGrind; Exam Prep / other formats in Astra if they help.

Does MicroGrind replace Astra AI?

We do not present it as a replacement. We highlight what MicroGrind has that Astra does not: Subject Map and 5–25 min sessions tied to it.

Any course?

Yes — primary through higher education.

Which files?

PDF, Word, text, photos. Web + PWA («Coming soon on Android»).
