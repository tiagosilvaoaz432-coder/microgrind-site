    Why MicroGrind

    • Map first. See the whole subject, chapter by chapter, before you study the first page.
    • Then short sessions. Pick 5 to 25 minutes and study the material organised in your map.
    • All in one place. Lessons, flashcards, quizzes, spaced repetition and mock exams, built from your own material.

    Common questions

    How much does MicroGrind cost?

    €4 a month. Open the app at microgrind.app, on the web or on your phone.

    Are the other apps’ prices exact?

    They are the cheapest paid plan we found in public sources on 6 November 2026. They change by country, store and promotion, so always check each app’s official site.

    Is the Subject Map a mind map?

    No. A mind map is a free-form diagram. The Subject Map organises your subject into chapters, topics, concepts and how they connect, from the material you upload, and it drives your short sessions.

    Can I use MicroGrind alongside another app?

    Yes. You can study your subject’s structure in MicroGrind and keep using another app for whatever else you need.

    What can I upload?

    PDFs, Word files, text and photos of your notes. The Subject Map is built from the file you upload.
