What Quizlet does well

  • A huge community library of sets — strong for vocabulary and card-based review.
  • Learn / Test / Study Guides on paid plans, when you already have (or can find) ready-made decks.

Choose MicroGrind if…

  • You want to see the structure of your subject — topics, concepts, definitions and relations — before you study.
  • You prefer short sessions of 5, 10, 15 or 25 minutes tied to a map built from your own PDF, Word, text or photos.
  • You want a Subject Map (structure of the subject), not only a community deck of cards.

Common questions

Is MicroGrind a mind map like Quizlet diagrams?

No. A mind map is a free-form visual diagram. A Subject Map organises your subject into topics, concepts, definitions and relations from your material, then powers short study sessions. Quizlet centres on flashcards and practice modes.

Can I use MicroGrind and Quizlet together?

Yes. You can study subject structure in MicroGrind and still review lists or vocabulary in Quizlet if that helps.

Does MicroGrind replace Quizlet / flashcards?

We do not present MicroGrind as a replacement. It fits when you want a Subject Map and timed sessions from your own material. Quizlet remains strong for community sets.

Does MicroGrind work for any course?

Yes — any course or subject, from primary to higher education. The map starts from the notes, handbook or slides you upload.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, Word, text and photos of notes. The app opens on the web; you can install it on your phone (PWA; «Coming soon on Android» per the site bar).
