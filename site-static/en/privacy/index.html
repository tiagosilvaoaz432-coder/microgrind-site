Privacy

Privacy and cookie policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

This policy covers the microgrind.app website and the web app at microgrind.app/app.

Who is responsible

MicroGrind is the controller for the data processed on this website.

Contact: ola@microgrind.app

What we collect

The website does not ask for your email or create an account. To use the app, open the web app or install the app on your phone.

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure how the website and web app are used: pages viewed, where the visit came from, device and browser type, and approximate country or region. The data is aggregated and we do not use it to identify you.

With the same consent, we also load OpenAI’s ad measurement pixel (ChatGPT Ads). It tells us whether our ads in ChatGPT work: it records when you open the web app from the site and when you create an account. It sends OpenAI those events, a random event identifier and technical visit data (page, browser, IP address). It does not send your email or your name.

We do not sell data and we do not pass it to other companies for marketing.

Cookies

  • Necessary. We store your cookie choice (mg-consent) and whether you closed the install prompt in your browser. They do not identify you and need no consent.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics). The _ga and _ga_* cookies are only set after you accept. They last up to 2 years. Until then, the site loads no Google script.
  • Advertising. We do not use any.

You can change your choice at any time under “Cookies” in the site footer. If you decline, we delete any analytics cookies already set.

Legal basis and Google

We use Google Analytics based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it whenever you like, without affecting what happened before.

Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Limited. Google may process data outside the European Union under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. More at policies.google.com/privacy.

The ad pixel is provided by OpenAI, which may process data outside the European Union under standard contractual clauses. More at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.

Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification or erasure of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent. You can also complain to the CNPD, the Portuguese data protection authority, at cnpd.pt.

To use these rights, email ola@microgrind.app.
