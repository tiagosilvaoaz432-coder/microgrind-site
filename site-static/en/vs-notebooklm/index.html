MicroGrind vs Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) — MicroGrind advantages

MicroGrind is a study app that builds a Subject Map — topics, concepts, definitions and relations — from your course notes, handbook or slides, then runs short study sessions of 5 to 25 minutes. It works for any course or subject, from primary school to higher education.

This page shows what MicroGrind has that Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) does not, using verifiable product facts. Note: Gemini Notebook offers Mind Maps — a visual diagram from your sources. That is not MicroGrind’s Subject Map.

What Gemini Notebook does well

  • Synthesises your sources: Audio Overviews, reports, quizzes, flashcards and artefacts from your documents.
  • Mind Maps and other formats to explore and summarise what you uploaded — strong as a research partner based on the sources you upload.

Choose MicroGrind if…

  • You want a Subject Map (structure of the subject), not a Mind Map.
  • You prefer fixed short sessions of 5–25 minutes from that map.
  • You want to study topic by topic with a mini-lesson, cards and questions in the same timed session.

Common questions

Is the Subject Map the same as Gemini Notebook’s Mind Map?

No. A Mind Map is a free-form / generated visual diagram from sources. A Subject Map organises the subject into topics, concepts, definitions and relations and powers sessions of 5 to 25 minutes. Similar names; different products.

Can I use both?

Yes. Many students use Gemini Notebook for synthesis or Audio Overview and MicroGrind for short structured sessions.

Does MicroGrind replace Gemini Notebook?

We do not present it as a replacement. We highlight Subject Map + short sessions — which Gemini Notebook is not.

Any course?

Yes — primary through higher education.

Which files?

PDF, Word, text, photos. Web + PWA («Coming soon on Android»).

Cost?

MicroGrind: N/A. Gemini Notebook: personal plan with limits; Plus/Pro/Ultra via Google AI — confirm on notebooklm.google/plans.
