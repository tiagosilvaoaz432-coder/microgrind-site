PDFs and notes

Study from your PDFs — map first, then study.

MicroGrind doesn't just turn a PDF into flashcards. It first builds a Subject Map of the document, then creates study sessions of 5–25 minutes from that map.

Why jumping from a PDF to questions falls short

Going straight from the file to questions can skip important parts, repeat concepts, mix topics and overweight details.

How it works

  1. Upload a PDF, a Word file, text or a photo of your notes.
  2. See your Subject Map.
  3. When you start a session, choose a duration between 5 and 25 minutes.
  4. Study the material organised in your Subject Map.

Built from your document

The Subject Map is built from the file you upload — notes, textbook or slides — for any course.

Common questions

Is it a mind map?

No. A mind map is a free-form visual diagram. A Subject Map organises your subject into topics, concepts, definitions and how they connect, using your own material. MicroGrind uses that structure to prepare short study sessions.

What can I upload?

PDFs, Word files, text and photos of your notes. The Subject Map is built from the file you upload.

Does it work on the web and on my phone?

Yes. Open it on the web, or install it on your phone.
