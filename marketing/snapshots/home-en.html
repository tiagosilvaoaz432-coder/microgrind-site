The principle

Being correct is not enough.

It has to be supported by your own document. MicroGrind builds the subject map from your course pack, textbook or notes.

Common questions

Does the AI invent things?

It’s the risk that worries us most. MicroGrind builds everything from your document, not from general knowledge. Being right is not enough: it has to be in your material.

What if my notes have mistakes?

MicroGrind stays faithful to the document you upload; it does not correct it. Use the best source you have, such as the textbook or your lecturer’s slides.

Does it work with dense textbooks and slides that were rushed through?

That’s what we designed it for. The map separates the material into chapters, topics and concepts so you can see how it fits together before you study.

I have little time and a lot of courses. Is this for me?

Sessions last between 5 and 25 minutes and fit between classes. Each session starts from the map, so you don’t spend time deciding where to begin.

Can I use it?

Yes. Open it on the web, or install it on your phone.
